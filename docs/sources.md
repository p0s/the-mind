### AI, Religious War, and Cyber Animism

[Joscha Bach with Vance Crowe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z7DYmHKSfOI) — 19 May 2026, Vance Crowe.

The publisher highlights hallucination, dreaming, and Bach's cyber-animism framing. Potentially useful alongside the self and spirituality questions; the interview's political and religious commentary is not adopted as the site's position.

### The age of AGE: Artificial General Experience

[Joscha Bach's essay](https://joscha.substack.com/p/the-age-of-age) — 27 March 2026.

Discussing Rana Gujral's *The AI Instinct*, this essay considers cognitive agency across multiple dimensions, grounding through interaction, and human–AI collaboration. It helps explain why a single capability score is a limited description of a mind. Its scenarios for future human–AI relationships are speculative context, not evidence that current systems have subjective experience.

Publisher chapter links include [the function of consciousness at 32:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C6IHcZbCMAM&t=1935s), [self-awareness at 48:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C6IHcZbCMAM&t=2903s), and [metacognition at 55:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C6IHcZbCMAM&t=3309s).

### Future Day 2026

[Joscha Bach, Anders Sandberg, and Adam Ford](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFeTS1RjBf4) — 2 March 2026, Science, Technology & the Future.

A multi-speaker event recording whose description identifies Bach's presentation on the machine consciousness hypothesis and a discussion with Sandberg. The full recording also contains Ford's separate presentation; it should not be treated as Bach-only material.

## Research-program context

These official CIMC sources explain how the hypothesis is being developed into a broader research program. Use them for orientation, not as replacements for primary theory citations or as evidence that a system is conscious.

### September 2026 research update

[CIMC: Berlin Recap, Hackathons & Upcoming Events](https://cimcai.substack.com/p/cimc-berlin-recap-hackathons-and) — 25 September 2026.

Includes a researcher note by Hikari Sorensen, a recap of the Berlin meeting, and links to Bach's recent podcast appearances. The note leaves open whether representational similarities between language models and human minds entail experience. At the time of this check, the Berlin talk recordings were announced as forthcoming, so they are not listed here as available recordings.

### AAAI 2026 recordings and proceedings

[CIMC's symposium report and recording links](https://cimcai.substack.com/p/aaai-2026-machine-consciousness-symposium) — 19 August 2026.

A guide to the April symposium's discussions of implementation, measurement, selfhood, and ethical uncertainty. The [AAAI proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI-SS/issue/view/734) include a machine-consciousness section with work by multiple authors and differing positions; these papers are broader research context, not collectively Bach's claims.
