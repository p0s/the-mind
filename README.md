# the-mind

**What is happening when you experience a world, feel like a self, or make a choice?**

Explore how perception, feelings, selfhood, and consciousness fit together, and what this could mean for AI.

This site offers a plain-language introduction to Joscha Bach and Hikari Sorensen’s *The Machine Consciousness Hypothesis*, with supporting ideas from Bach’s talks and interviews. It explains the proposed model, the reasoning behind it, and the questions it leaves open.
